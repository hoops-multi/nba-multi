    What do Heat, Edge and Consensus mean?

    Edge is our probability minus what the price implies; positive means we think the bookie is paying too much. Heat is our 0-100 rating of the leg from its probability, the edge over the price, tipster agreement and the matchup. Consensus is how many of the sources that tipped that market agree with the leg (3 of 3 means every source that published a call on it took this side; n/a means nobody tipped it, so it rests on our own read).
